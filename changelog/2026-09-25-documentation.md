The docs have been rewritten from the ground up and checked against the code.

- **Grouped navigation:** Get started, Build and operate, Developers, Platform,
  and Help, with a new landing page to find the right place fast.
- **Step-by-step guides** for setting up your organization, working with projects,
  securing your organization with 2FA and SSO, and automating with tokens, the API,
  and MCP. Each shows the dashboard, API, and assistant way side by side.
- **Architecture pages** that explain how Flagon fits together, from the request
  lifecycle to tenant isolation, the permissions model, and the shared tool
  registry.
- **Reference pages** for every scope, role, audit action, configuration option,
  and limit, plus new security, self-hosting, and troubleshooting sections.

Start at the [docs home](/docs) or the
[architecture overview](/docs/architecture/overview).
