The core objects you create and manage inside an organization are here.

- **Projects** are the deployable unit, with a README, repository link, and
  soft delete with restore.
- **Project access.** A five-rung collaborator ladder (read, triage, write,
  maintain, admin) layered on an org-wide **base permission**, so access is
  simple by default and precise when you need it.
- **Teams** group members, with maintainers who manage the roster, and hold
  project roles that every member inherits.
- **Ownership.** Users or teams can own a project, a tier above admin that
  controls deleting, restoring, and transferring it.

Everything is available from the dashboard, the API, the assistant, and MCP. See
[Projects](/docs/platform/projects), [Teams](/docs/platform/teams), and
[Roles & permissions](/docs/platform/roles).
