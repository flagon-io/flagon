---
title: Single sign-on and required two-factor
date: 2026-09-19
tag: Shipped
area: Platform
---

Organizations can now control how their members sign in.

- **Single sign-on.** Connect an identity provider over OIDC or SAML. Members
  sign in through it, new people are provisioned into the organization on their
  first sign-in, and one Flagon account can belong to several SSO organizations
  at once. Owners can require SSO for every member.
- **Required two-factor.** Owners and admins can require every member to have
  two-factor authentication enabled before they can reach the organization.

See [Single sign-on](/docs/platform/sso) and
[Two-factor authentication](/docs/platform/two-factor).
