# Projects

<Callout tone="note" title="Coming soon">
Projects are not available yet. Today flags are organization-global: every flag,
key, and resource lives directly under your [organization](/docs/platform/organizations).
This page describes where projects are headed so you can plan for them.
</Callout>

A **project** will group the work for one system or team inside an
[organization](/docs/platform/organizations). It's how a large workspace stays
navigable instead of being one flat list of every flag, key, and resource.

Think of a project as the home for everything that belongs to a single app or
service: its feature flags first, then the other products attached to it as the
platform grows. Projects are also the unit the [Catalog](/docs/catalog) will
organize services around.

## Working in projects

Once projects land:

- Each organization will be able to have as many projects as you need.
- Resources will belong to a project, so switching projects scopes what you see
  to that system's work.
- Projects live under the organization, so [members and roles](/docs/platform/organizations)
  carry across them: you won't re-invite your team per project.

<Callout tone="note" title="Environments are shared">
Projects organize *what* you're working on; [environments](/docs/platform/environments)
(Production, Preview, Development) are the *stages* each project moves through.
The two compose: a project's flags are configured per environment.
</Callout>
