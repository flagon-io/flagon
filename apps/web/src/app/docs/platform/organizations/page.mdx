# Organizations & teams

An **organization** is your workspace on Flagon. It owns your projects and
resources, it's the boundary for billing, and it's the team of people who can
work in it. Every resource on the platform belongs to exactly one organization,
and access is always scoped to it.

You can belong to more than one organization, and switch between them from the
console.

## Members and roles

People you add to an organization become **members**, each with a role that
decides what they can do.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including transferring ownership and deleting the organization. Exactly one per organization. |
| **Admin** | Manage members, tokens, and all product configuration. |
| **Member** | Work within the products (create and configure flags, and so on). |

### One owner

Every organization has exactly **one owner**. Ownership isn't a role you assign
or invite someone into: it moves by **transfer**. The current owner hands
ownership to another member, and in the same step steps down to admin. That keeps
accountability unambiguous, there's always exactly one person who ultimately
controls the organization.

When you invite people, the roles you can give them are **admin** and **member**.

## Invitations

Add teammates by sending an **invitation** to their email from the organization's
members settings. They accept, sign in, and become a member with the role you
chose. Because [pricing is usage-based](/docs/platform#principles), there's no
per-seat cost, invite the whole team.

## Signing in

Flagon supports multiple verified email addresses per user, so you can sign in
with any address you've added and verified, handy when your work and personal
accounts blur. Your primary address is the one shown to teammates.
