# Catalog <Badge tone="soon">Coming soon</Badge>

The **Catalog** is the heart of Flagon. It's a live map of everything you run:
your services and components, who owns them, which environments they're deployed
to, and the products (feature flags, deployments, and more) attached to each,
all in one place, on one foundation.

Where [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags) is a product you use, the Catalog is
the fabric the whole platform hangs on: it's how a service, a team, and the
things that act on that service stay connected instead of scattered across a
dozen tools.

<Callout tone="note" title="On the way">
The Catalog is the centerpiece of what Flagon is becoming, and it's in active
development. Feature Flags is available today and is built on the same
foundation: the [organizations](/docs/platform/organizations),
[projects](/docs/platform/projects), and [environments](/docs/platform/environments)
the Catalog will bring together. This page will grow as it ships.
</Callout>

## The idea

Internal developer platforms tend to become another silo: a wiki no one updates,
a spreadsheet of services, ownership that's true until someone changes teams. The
Catalog is meant to be the opposite: derived from the same primitives your
products already use, so it's correct because it's *live*, not because someone
remembered to update it.

- **Services and components**: what you run, described once.
- **Ownership**: which [team](/docs/platform/organizations) owns each thing, so
  a page or a rollout always has an owner.
- **Environments**: where each service is deployed, shared with every product.
- **Attached products**: the flags, deployments, and resources acting on a
  service, all reachable from the service itself.
