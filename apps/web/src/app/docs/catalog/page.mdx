# Catalog

The **Catalog** is the heart of Flagon. It's a live map of everything you run:
your projects, the teams that own them, which environments they're deployed to,
and the products (feature flags today, more to come) attached to each, all in one
place, on one foundation.

Where [Feature Flags](/docs/feature-flags) is a product you use, the Catalog is
the fabric the whole platform hangs on: it's how a project, a team, and the
things that act on that project stay connected instead of scattered across a
dozen tools.

## What's here today

The Catalog is live. You can:

- **Create projects** and describe them: a name, a description, a lifecycle stage
  (planning through GA), a criticality tier, and a rendered README.
- **Organize people into [teams](/docs/platform/teams)** and hand each team
  ownership of the projects it's responsible for, so every project has a clear
  owner. Grant other teams access, GitHub-repository style.
- **Tag and browse** your projects as a single catalog, visible to everyone in
  the [organization](/docs/platform/organizations).

<Callout tone="note" title="Still on the way">
Deeper catalog surfaces are in active development: dependencies between projects,
scorecards and maturity checks, systems and domains grouping, and linking a
project back to its source repository. The foundation below is shipped today, and
this page grows as those land.
</Callout>

## The idea

Internal developer platforms tend to become another silo: a wiki no one updates,
a spreadsheet of services, ownership that's true until someone changes teams. The
Catalog is meant to be the opposite: derived from the same primitives your
products already use, so it's correct because it's *live*, not because someone
remembered to update it.

- **Projects**: what you run, described once.
- **Ownership**: which [team](/docs/platform/teams) owns each thing, so a page or
  a rollout always has an owner.
- **Environments**: where each project is deployed, shared with every product.
- **Attached products**: the flags and resources acting on a project, all
  reachable from the project itself.
