Single sign-on, required two-factor, scoped tokens, and the audit log have
shipped. What's left is the lifecycle around them: keeping an organization's
roster in step with its identity provider automatically, and giving owners
control over how long, and where, their members stay signed in.

- SCIM provisioning and deprovisioning from your identity provider
- Org session policies: session lifetime and idle timeouts
- Sign members out of an organization everywhere, at once
- Built on the same roles, scopes, and audit trail as the rest of the platform
