Who can do what, proven rather than assumed. Role-based access across the org and each product, backed by tenant isolation and a full audit trail, with the account protections teams expect.

- Org-wide and per-product roles and permissions
- Single sign-on and two-factor authentication
- Org security policies and session controls
- Scoped access tokens for programmatic use
- An audit log of every sensitive action
