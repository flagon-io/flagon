The home base every tenant lives in: organizations, the people who belong to them, and the roles that decide what each can do. Multi-tenant from the first commit, with every tenant's data isolated at the database itself.

- Organizations as the top-level tenant
- People, membership, and ownership
- Roles that decide who can do what
- Tenant isolation enforced in the database with row-level security
