Metering turned into plans and limits. AI usage is already metered per organization, with a daily cap enforced today; this turns that into a usage dashboard, transparent plan limits, and billing, priced to sit below what it costs to run.

- A usage dashboard for every organization
- Plan limits and enforcement beyond today's free-plan caps
- Transparent, honest pricing
- Billing, payment methods, and invoicing
