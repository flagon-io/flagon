The heart of Flagon: your projects and teams stay in sync with the systems those definitions already live in, in both directions. Define once, and let it flow to and from the tools you already use.

- Two-way sync, not one-way import
- Connect the systems your definitions already live in
- Changes reconcile in both directions
- Built on the same API and permissions model as everything else
