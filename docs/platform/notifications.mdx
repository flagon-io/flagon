# Notifications

Notifications are your personal feed of activity across the organizations you
belong to - an invite you accepted, a member who joined, a role that changed.
They are **personal**: a notification belongs to you, not to an organization.
That makes them separate from the [audit log](/platform/audit-log), which is the
organization's shared, immutable record of who did what.

## Where they show up

- The **bell** in the top bar shows a badge with your unread count and a short
  feed. The feed is **unread first, then most recent**, so an item you haven't
  read yet is never buried under newer ones you have - even if it's weeks old.
- **Settings → Notifications** is the full feed, with **All** and **Unread**
  filters, plus your delivery preferences. It's a personal surface (your account,
  spanning every organization you belong to), also reachable from the bell's
  **View all**.

## Reading a notification

Selecting a notification opens its detail rather than jumping you somewhere:

- It **marks the notification read** (its `read_at` timestamp records when).
- From the detail you can **mark it unread** again to bring it back to the top of
  the feed, or **open** its linked page when it has one.
- **Mark all as read** clears the whole unread count at once.

Opening a notification never navigates on its own - you follow the link only when
you choose to, so a click is never a surprise jump.

## Delivery

Today notifications are delivered **in-product** (the bell and the feed). Email
delivery is planned.

## With the AI and the API

Everything above is available through the [API](/api) and to the
[AI assistant](/ai) and [MCP](/mcp-hub): list your notifications, mark one read
or unread, or mark them all read. As with every mutation, the in-product agent
proposes the change and you confirm it.
