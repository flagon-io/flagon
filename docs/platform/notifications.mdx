title: Notifications

**Notifications** are your personal feed of things that happened to you across
the organizations you belong to: you were added to an org, your role changed,
someone accepted your invitation, you were given access to a project. They are
**personal**: a notification belongs to you, not to an organization, and only you
can see or change it. That makes them separate from the
[audit log](/docs/platform/audit-log), the organization's shared, immutable
record of who did what.

## What you are notified about

| Type | When |
| --- | --- |
| `org.created` | You created an organization. |
| `org.member_added` | Someone added you to an organization (directly, or by inviting an email that already has an account). |
| `org.role_changed` | Your organization role changed. |
| `org.member_removed` | You were removed from an organization. |
| `org.invite_accepted` | Someone accepted an invitation you sent. |
| `org.deleted` | An organization you belong to was deleted. |
| `project.access_granted` | You were given a role on a project. |
| `project.access_changed` | Your role on a project changed. |
| `team.member_added` | You were added to a team. |

## Where they show up

- The **bell** in the top bar shows your unread count and a short feed. The feed
  is **unread first, then newest**, so an unread item is never buried under newer
  ones you have already read.
- **Settings > Notifications** (`app.flagon.io/settings/notifications`) is the
  full feed with **All** and **Unread** tabs. It spans every organization you
  belong to.

## Reading a notification

Selecting a notification opens its detail rather than jumping you somewhere:

- It **marks the notification read** (its `read_at` timestamp records when).
- From the detail you can **mark it unread** again, or **open** its linked page
  when it has one.
- **Mark all as read** clears the whole unread count at once.

A click never navigates on its own; you follow the link only when you choose to.

## Delivery

Notifications are delivered **in-product** only (the bell and the feed).
Invitation emails are a separate thing: they go to people who are not members
yet (see [Members and invitations](/docs/platform/members)).

## Operations

Everything above is available through the API, the
[assistant](/docs/ai/assistant), and [MCP](/docs/ai/mcp):

| Operation | API | Tool | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| List your notifications | `GET /notifications` | `list_notifications` | `notifications` |
| Count unread | `GET /notifications/unread-count` | `count_unread_notifications` | `notifications` |
| Mark one read | `POST /notifications/{id}/read` | `mark_notification_read` | `notifications` |
| Mark one unread | `POST /notifications/{id}/unread` | `mark_notification_unread` | `notifications` |
| Mark all read | `POST /notifications/read-all` | `mark_all_notifications_read` | `notifications` |

`GET /notifications` returns up to `limit` items, unread first and then newest
(default 30; a value of 0 or less means 30, and anything above 100 is capped at
100). The `list_notifications` tool takes the same `limit`. As with every
change, the in-product assistant proposes marking notifications read and you
confirm it.

```bash
curl https://api.flagon.io/notifications/unread-count \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN"
```
