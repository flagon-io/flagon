A **team** is a named group of members inside an organization. Instead of
granting project access one person at a time, you build a team once and grant
*the team* a role on the projects it needs. Every member of the team inherits
that access, and when someone joins or leaves the team, their project reach
follows automatically.

Teams live under **Teams** (`app.flagon.io/<org>/teams`). Only organization
owners and admins create and delete teams; the person who creates a team becomes
its first maintainer.

## Team roles

A team has two internal roles that govern the team itself. They are separate
from any access the team holds on a project.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Maintainer** | Manage the team's membership (add and remove members, choose who is a maintainer) and edit the team's name, slug, and description. |
| **Member** | Belong to the team and inherit the project access granted to it. |

Organization owners and admins can always manage any team, whether or not they
are on it. Deleting a team is reserved for them: a maintainer runs a team but
does not disband it.

## How teams get project access

A team carries no project access on its own. You grant it a **project role**,
one rung of the collaborator ladder (`read`, `triage`, `write`, `maintain`, or
`admin`), on a specific project, and every team member gains that role there. A
team can also be made a project **owner**, which makes every member an owner of
that project. See [Project access](/docs/platform/project-access) for what each
rung and ownership can do.

- **A team can hold access to many projects.** Grant the same team `write` on
  one project and `admin` on another; each grant is independent.
- **A project can be shared with many teams.** A project lists the teams it is
  shared with alongside its individual collaborators.

Because a person's effective role on a project is the **highest** of all their
grants, being on a team can only ever raise someone's access on a project, never
lower it.

## Creating and managing a team

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Dashboard">

On **Teams**, choose **New team**, give it a name (the slug is derived from it
unless you set one) and an optional description. Open the team to add members,
change who is a maintainer, and see the projects it can reach. Grant a team
access to a project from that project's **Settings > Access**.

</Tab>

<Tab title="API">

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.flagon.io/orgs/acme/teams \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "Platform", "description": "Runs the shared infrastructure"}'

curl -X POST https://api.flagon.io/orgs/acme/teams/platform/members \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"login": "dev@example.com", "role": "member"}'
```

</Tab>

<Tab title="Assistant">

Ask: "Create a Platform team and add dev@example.com to it." The assistant
proposes `create_team` and then `add_team_member`; each runs when you confirm.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

People you add to a team must already be members of the organization: a team
elevates existing members, it never brings in outsiders. For a walkthrough, see
[Organize with teams](/docs/guides/organize-with-teams).

Deleting a team removes it from the organization, and its project grants and
ownerships stop having effect immediately. The team is soft-deleted in the
database, but there is no restore operation, so treat deletion as final.

Team changes are audited: `team.created`, `team.updated`, `team.deleted`,
`team.member_added`, `team.member_role_changed`, and `team.member_removed`.

## Operations

| Operation | API | Tool | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| List teams | `GET /orgs/{slug}/teams` | `list_teams` | `read:team` |
| Read a team | `GET /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}` | `get_team` | `read:team` |
| Create a team | `POST /orgs/{slug}/teams` | `create_team` | `write:team` |
| Edit a team | `PATCH /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}` | `update_team` | `write:team` |
| Delete a team | `DELETE /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}` | `delete_team` | `admin:team` |
| List team members | `GET /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}/members` | `list_team_members` | `read:team` |
| Add a team member | `POST /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}/members` | `add_team_member` | `write:team` |
| Change a team role | `PUT /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}/members/{userId}/role` | `set_team_member_role` | `write:team` |
| Remove a team member | `DELETE /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}/members/{userId}` | `remove_team_member` | `write:team` |
| List a team's projects | `GET /orgs/{slug}/teams/{team}/projects` | `list_team_projects` | `read:team` |

Granting a team a role on a project, or making it an owner, is a project
operation and requires `admin:project` (see
[Project access](/docs/platform/project-access)). Scopes are a ceiling on top of
your role, and tenant isolation is enforced in the database.
