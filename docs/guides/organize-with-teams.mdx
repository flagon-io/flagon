---
title: Organize with teams
description: Group organization members into teams, choose who maintains each team, and grant a whole team a role on a project.
---

A **team** is a named group of organization members, such as `platform` or `payments`. Teams
exist so you can grant access once and have it follow people: give the `platform` team `write`
on a project, and everyone on the team gets `write`; add someone to the team later and they
inherit it automatically. Removing them from the team removes the access that came from it.

By the end of this guide you will have a team with members and a maintainer, and that team will
hold a role on at least one project.

<Callout type="info" title="Before you start">

- **Create or delete a team:** organization **owner** or **admin**.
- **Edit a team or manage its members:** organization owner or admin, or a **maintainer** of
  that team.
- **Grant a team a role on a project:** effective **admin** on that project (organization
  owners and admins always qualify). See [Manage project access](/docs/guides/manage-project-access).
- **API token scopes:** `write:team` to create teams and manage members, `admin:team` to delete
  teams, `admin:project` to grant a team project access.

</Callout>

## Team roles

A team has its own two roles, separate from organization roles and project roles:

| Team role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| `maintainer` | Edit the team's name, slug, and description, and add, remove, or change the role of its members. |
| `member` | Belongs to the team and receives every project grant the team holds. |

Whoever creates a team becomes its first maintainer, so every team starts with someone who can
manage it. A maintainer cannot delete the team; only organization owners and admins can.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Create the team">

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Dashboard">

1. Open **Teams** in the sidebar and select **New team**.
2. Enter a **Team name**, and optionally a **Slug** and **Description**.
3. Select **Create team**. Flagon opens the team's page with **Members**, **Projects**, and **Settings** tabs.

</Tab>

<Tab title="API">

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.flagon.io/orgs/acme/teams \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"name": "Platform", "description": "Runs shared infrastructure"}'
```

The slug is derived from the name when omitted (`platform`). A `201` returns the team.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Assistant">

> Create a team called Platform in acme.

The assistant proposes **Confirm: Create team "Platform" in "acme"**.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

</Step>

<Step title="Add members">

Team members must already be members of the organization. [Invite them](/docs/guides/invite-your-team)
first if they aren't.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Dashboard">

On the team's **Members** tab, add a member by **Email or username** and pick a **Role**
(`maintainer` or `member`). Change a role or remove someone from the same table.

</Tab>

<Tab title="API">

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.flagon.io/orgs/acme/teams/platform/members \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"login": "dana@example.com", "role": "member"}'
```

`role` is required. Change it later with
`PUT /orgs/acme/teams/platform/members/{userId}/role` and a body of `{"role": "maintainer"}`,
or remove the person with `DELETE /orgs/acme/teams/platform/members/{userId}`. Get `userId`
from `GET /orgs/acme/teams/platform/members`.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Assistant">

> Add dana@example.com to the platform team as a maintainer.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

</Step>

<Step title="Give the team a project role">

A team grant uses the project role ladder: `read`, `triage`, `write`, `maintain`, `admin`.

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Dashboard">

Either open the project and go to **Settings > Access**, then **Add team** in the teams section;
or open the team's **Projects** tab and add a project there. Choose the **Team** (or
**Project**) and a **Role**.

</Tab>

<Tab title="API">

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.flagon.io/orgs/acme/projects/checkout/teams \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLAGON_TOKEN" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"team": "platform", "role": "write"}'
```

Change the role with `PUT /orgs/acme/projects/checkout/teams/platform/role`, or revoke it with
`DELETE /orgs/acme/projects/checkout/teams/platform`.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Assistant">

> Give the platform team write access to the checkout project.

The assistant proposes **Confirm: Grant team "platform" write on project "checkout" in
"acme"**.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

</Step>

<Step title="Verify it worked">

- The team's **Projects** tab lists the project and the role.
- `GET /orgs/acme/teams/platform/projects` returns `{"projects": [...]}` with the granted role.
- `GET /orgs/acme/projects/checkout/teams` returns the team in `{"teams": [...]}`.
- The audit log records `team.created`, `team.member_added`, and
  `project.team_access_granted`.

A member's access to the project is now at least the team's role. If they also have an
organization base permission or a direct grant, the highest one wins.

</Step>

</Steps>

## Deleting a team

Deleting a team (organization owners and admins, `admin:team`) frees its slug and removes every
project grant and project ownership the team held, so members lose any access that came only
from the team. From the dashboard use the team's **Settings** tab; from the API,
`DELETE /orgs/acme/teams/platform`.

## Common failures

| Status | `detail` | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `409` | `a team with that slug already exists` | Choose another name or slug. |
| `409` | `that user must be an organization member first` | Invite the person to the organization, then add them to the team. |
| `409` | `that user is already on this team` | Change their role instead. |
| `409` | `that team already has a role on this project` | Change the team's role instead of adding it again. |
| `404` | `team not found` | Check the team slug. Deleted teams are not found. |
| `404` | `no user with that email or username` | Check the login; the person needs a Flagon account and organization membership. |
| `403` | `you don't have permission to do that` | You are not an owner, admin, or maintainer of this team, or not an admin on the project. |
