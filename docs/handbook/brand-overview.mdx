Brand is not the logo. The logo is a small part of it. Brand is the total of every experience someone has with Flagon: the first paragraph they read on the site, the shape of an error message, how fast we answer a question in the Discord, whether the pricing did what we said it would. Every one of those leaves an impression, and the sum of all of them is the brand. We do not get to decide what it is. We only get to decide what we put in.

We take it seriously for two plain reasons.

**It is how we grow.** We are not going to out-spend anyone on ads, and we do not want to. People recommend Flagon because using it felt honest and the work looked good. A brand people trust is a brand people bring up in a thread without being asked. That is the growth we are building for, and it compounds.

**Trust is slow in and fast out.** It takes a long time to earn someone's belief that we mean what we write, and one lazy headline, one dark pattern, one canned support reply to spend it. So the bar is not "did we ship something." The bar is "would this hold up if a skeptical person looked closely." They will look closely. We want them to.

## The golden rule

Everything in these pages comes back to one idea we use everywhere else too: treat people the way we would want to be treated. If a tactic would give us the ick as a customer, it gives them the ick, so we do not use it, even when it would work. Hold a brand decision up to that and most of them get easy.
