If you've read this far, you know what we're trying to build and why. A company built in the open only works if people take part, so here are concrete ways to do that.

## Use it, and tell us the truth

The single most useful thing you can do is use what we make and tell us where it falls short. Not polite feedback, the real kind: what confused you, what broke, what you expected that wasn't there. We treat that as a gift, not a complaint. The [Discord](https://discord.gg/dtYQs6rPXN) is open, and so are the [issues](https://github.com/flagon-io).

## Build in the open with us

This whole thing is open source, handbook included. Fix a typo, sharpen a page, file an issue, open a pull request. If a page here is wrong or unclear, that's a bug you're welcome to fix. See [open source and building in public](/handbook/open-source) for how we work with contributors.

## Send the right people our way

If Flagon would genuinely help someone you know, tell them. Word of mouth from someone who means it is the best thing that can happen to us, and we don't take it for granted.

## Come build it

If reading this made you want to work somewhere like this, that's not an accident. Have a look at [careers](/careers), and at [who Flagon is for, and who it isn't](/not-for-everyone) so you can tell whether you'd actually love it here.

## Hold us to all of this

Every page in this book is a standard we've written down on purpose, so you can hold us to it. If we fall short, say so, [here](https://github.com/flagon-io) or at [hey@flagon.io](mailto:hey@flagon.io). Being kept honest is the most valuable help of all.
