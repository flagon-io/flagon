Flagon is a self-hostable developer platform for operating your engineering system. Today you use it
to run **organizations**, the **projects** inside them, and the **people and teams** who work on
them, with access control, single sign-on, and an audit log built in. Every capability works three
ways: in the dashboard at [app.flagon.io](https://app.flagon.io), over the REST API at
`https://api.flagon.io`, and through an AI assistant (in the product or over MCP) that acts as you
and asks before it changes anything.

## Key concepts

An **organization** is the tenant: it owns projects, members, teams, tokens, and settings, and no
data crosses between organizations. A **project** is a deployable app, the core unit you work on.
**Members** hold an org role (owner, admin, or member), and each project resolves their effective
**project role** (read, triage, write, maintain, or admin) from the org's base permission, direct
grants, and team grants. **Access tokens** let scripts and agents act with a subset of that access,
limited by **scopes**. Mutations are recorded in the org's **audit log**.

See [Core concepts](/docs/get-started/core-concepts) for every term and how they fit together.
