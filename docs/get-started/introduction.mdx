Flagon is a **developer platform** for operating your whole system: a single
place to bring your projects and tools together, understand what's happening
across them, and act on it, from the dashboard, the API, or an AI assistant. It's
powerful with AI, and great without it.

Use it hosted at [app.flagon.io](https://app.flagon.io), or run the whole thing
yourself. It is open source and self-hostable end to end.

## The idea

Your work is spread across a dozen tools. Flagon's job is to pull that together
into one system you can operate and reason about, with AI wired through all of it.
The point is not another dashboard to check; it's to ask
_"what changed across everything this week?"_ and get a real answer, grounded in
your actual data.

Everything runs on a few ideas:

- **One place, many surfaces.** Anything you can do in the dashboard you can do by
  asking the [assistant](/docs/ai/assistant), or over the
  [API](/docs/api/using-the-api) and [MCP](/docs/ai/mcp). Same operations, same
  permissions, three front doors.
- **AI over your real data, safely.** The assistant acts as you, with your scopes,
  and changes are proposed for you to confirm. AI is a first-class way to operate
  Flagon, not a bolt-on.
- **Open and yours.** Self-hostable, permission-scoped, and built in the open.

## What you can do today

- Create and manage **organizations** and invite **members** with scoped roles.
- Create **projects** to organize the systems you operate.
- Mint **API tokens** with fine-grained **scopes**, and drive everything
  programmatically or over MCP.
- Ask the **assistant** to do any of the above, and to read these docs, for you.

## Where it's going

Flagon is growing into the hub for your whole engineering system: connected
context across your tools, metrics and insights, agents and orchestration with
humans in the loop, and the governance to run AI safely over real data. Those
areas are marked _Soon_ in the navigation as they land.
